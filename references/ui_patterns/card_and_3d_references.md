# Sourced UI & 3D Interaction Reference (Detective Research)

## 1. Benchmarked Platform: Allrecipes (`allrecipes.com`)
- **Background**: Crisp daylight white (`#ffffff`) and warm cream (`#fbf9f5`). Absolutely no dark/cyberpunk mode — food requires natural light to feel appetizing, clean, and delicious to everyday home cooks.
- **Brand Accents**: Warm terracotta orange (`#e25b27`) paired with a navy trust bar (`#1e3a8a`) and warm golden saffron (`#f59e0b`).
- **Typography & Tone**: Friendly, accessible, household food magazine aesthetic. Bold, clear headings with star ratings and reviews (`★★★★★ 4.9 (840)`).

## 2. 3D Culinary Model: The Layered Rotating Smashed Burger
- **Object**: 3D detailed burger in Three.js on a bright culinary studio counter.
- **Layers (Bottom to Top)**:
  1. Bottom toasted brioche bun (`#d97706`)
  2. Dill pickle slices (`#15803d`)
  3. Seared juicy beef patty (`#3d2014`)
  4. Melted cheddar cheese draped in a diamond orientation with drooping corners (`#f59e0b`)
  5. Ruffled crisp green lettuce leaves (`#22c55e`)
  6. Two overlapping ripe red tomato slices (`#dc2626`)
  7. Golden brioche top bun with 45+ toasted sesame seeds scattered across the dome surface (`#fef3c7`)
- **Lighting & Dynamics**: Soft daylight ambient lighting + warm key light casting a soft circular ground shadow. Smooth 360-degree rotation responsive to mouse tilt.
