Product Experience, Emotional Design & Platform Feed Protocol:
- Emotional & Psychological Design: The website must capture the visceral emotions of food and cravings. Compare top competitors and viral culinary platforms to decode the psychological factors that captivate users and trigger appetite. Implement sensory-driven color palettes (warm, rich, appetizing tones), tactile depth, and lighting that highlight taste and freshness.
- Platform Architecture (Discovery Feed + Search): The product is not merely a recipe search bar; it is a full culinary discovery platform. The architecture features an engaging AI search/investigation zone at the top, coupled with an active, dynamic discovery feed below displaying tempting recipe cards.
- Mandatory Image Scraping: Every recipe card in the feed and search results must feature scraped, high-appeal food imagery. Showing text alone is not enough; visual craveability is essential to retaining user attention and elevating the experience.
- Dynamic Feed Experience: The feed must be alive and dynamic, serving an engaging variety of trending, leaked, or mouthwatering recipes that entice users to explore, click, and cook.
